ViaNexta08 Tailwinds & headwinds

Signals shaping the next 12 months

Opportunity
AI took 48% of all 2025 VC and vertical AI overtook horizontal in Q4. Strong appetite for a vertical CPG play. (CB Insights)
Opportunity
Only ~10% of consumer-goods firms have integrated AI agents, while ~94% plan to within two years. First-inning timing. (BCG)
Opportunity
Tariffs and private label are squeezing margins, pushing brands to rethink sourcing. (McKinsey, Circana)
Watch
Incumbents are widening fast: Keychain expanding to the UK and retailer private-label, AI-native ERPs (Doss, $55M) pulling big rounds nearby. (TechCrunch)
Watch
Direct rivals are funding up: Corvera +$4.2M, Jampack scaling volume. The emerging-brand lane is now contested. (Tech.eu)
ViaNexta09 How this gets built

The pipeline behind the dashboard

Scan
A Python competitor-monitor scanner pulls funding announcements and market signals from public sources (TechCrunch, PRNewswire, Tech.eu, CB Insights, McKinsey, BCG) on a recurring cadence.
Verify
Every figure is checked against a primary source before it lands in the report; no funding number ships without a citable announcement behind it.
Structure
Findings get organized into a positioning matrix, funding comparison, and threat/overlap table: the same three views a strategy team would build by hand, generated automatically.
Publish
A report generator renders the structured data straight into this interactive, scroll-snap deck, with no manual slide-building for each refresh.
